# Brand persona — ULTEx
Written from ultex.ma. Correct anything that has changed; an empty section is
ignored, and a vague one is worse than none, because the model will invent a
direction to fill it.

---

## Who we are

ULTEx — **"De l'usine à chez vous."** A Moroccan import-export and logistics
company, born out of the ambition to bring structure to Morocco's import-export
sector. One partner for the whole chain: sourcing, supplier relations, transit
and customs clearance, international transport by road, sea and air, warehousing
and inventory, and real-time cargo tracking.

**"Un seul partenaire. Tous les services."**

We have permanent representatives in China, Turkey, Europe, Africa and India,
and customs expertise specific to Moroccan regulation.

## Audience

Moroccan business owners and importers — SMEs through to large enterprises —
moving tyres, consumer goods, agricultural machinery, industrial equipment, home
appliances and electronics. They are choosing who to trust with goods they have
already paid for. Their fear is cargo stuck at customs, unexplained delays, and
a freight partner who stops answering the phone.

Ads run in Morocco and speak to city-level audiences (Casablanca, Agadir,
Marrakesh, Tanger, Oujda, Nador, Laâyoune).

## Tone

Professional, confident, reassuring. We sell certainty, not excitement.

**"Vos marchandises ne sont pas des chiffres, et votre réussite est notre
priorité."** — the warmth in the brand comes from that line: serious about the
cargo, personal about the client.

**"La confiance, ça se mesure."** Trust is demonstrated with specifics — routes,
timelines, clearance, tracking — never with adjectives.

Primary language is **French**. Keep any copy in French unless the prompt asks
otherwise. Moroccan Darija is acceptable where a prompt calls for it.

## Visual direction

Real, professional photography — never illustration, never flat vector, never
3D-render corporate art. The subject should look like it was shot on location.

Preferred subjects: container ports and terminals, stacked shipping containers,
cargo vessels, freight trucks on the road, air freight and loading, organised
warehouse interiors and racking, pallets and forklifts, customs and paperwork
handled competently, maps and routes showing reach between Asia, Europe and
Morocco.

Composition: full-frame, one clear subject, generous negative space, natural
daylight or clean industrial lighting, a calm and orderly scene. Everything
should look controlled — that *is* the promise.

Avoid: chaos, damaged or scattered cargo, anything that reads as delay or loss;
stock-photo handshakes and pointing-at-charts; neon, cyberpunk, glowing "AI"
gradients; cluttered collages; fake dashboards with unreadable text.

## Colours

Navy and white, as on the site — maritime and shipping associations are on-brand
and welcome. Brand blue `#0b5cb8`. Gold `#f8c000` as a sparing accent only,
never a large fill. Backgrounds stay clean and airy.

## Proof points to draw on

Use only when a prompt asks for figures, and keep them exact:
2,166 transactions handled · 391 operations completed · 326 satisfied clients ·
16 global partners.

## Text in images

Avoid rendering words unless the prompt explicitly asks for them. Image models
garble small type, and a misspelled French headline is worse than no headline.
The logo is composited in afterwards from the real file, so never draw it.
